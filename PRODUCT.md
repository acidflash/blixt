# Product

## Register
product

## Users and purpose
Blixt is a Swedish map for following lightning and weather on a phone or computer. Users need room to explore the map, understandable data sources and quick access to weather layers.

## Personality and principles
Calm, clear and practical. Preserve the existing dark map identity. Prioritize the map, readable information and predictable controls. On small screens, disclose layers and explanations in a compact panel. Avoid decorative dashboards, permanent panels covering the map and icon-only controls without accessible names.

## Accessibility
Support touch, mouse and keyboard. Use at least 44 px control targets, visible focus, readable contrast, text explanations of data colors and reduced-motion preferences. Report loading and failures without announcing every incoming strike.
